Questions

Frequently asked

Is this affiliated with JoSAA or NTA?

No. Counselling Wallah is an independent guidance platform, not affiliated with any government counselling authority.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We show historical, estimate-based possibilities — final admission depends on official counselling rules and seat availability.

Is basic search free?

Yes — basic college search and limited results are always free.

What about CSAB special rounds?

CSAB fills seats left vacant after JoSAA's regular rounds at NITs/IIITs/GFTIs. CSAB doesn't publish a bulk data file — cutoffs are looked up per institute/branch on csab.nic.in. This tool doesn't yet include CSAB-specific figures; check csab.nic.in directly for special-round cutoffs.

Where does the cutoff data come from?

Closing ranks are sourced from JoSAA 2025/2026 official round data (cross-checked via Careers360 and cracku.in reporting) where marked; unmarked branches use trend-based estimates. Fee and placement figures remain illustrative. See the Cutoffs tab on any college page for per-branch sourcing.
