B.Tech Admissions (2026)
Admissions to B.Tech degree programs in Chemical Science & Technology, Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mathematics and Computing, and Mechanical Engineering at NIT Mizoram in the year 2026 are only through seats allocated during Central Counseling conducted by The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) and Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB).
As announced through JoSAA 2026 schedule, there will be five rounds of seat allocation. After the JoSAA rounds conclude, any vacant seats in the NIT+ System will be filled through CSAB Special Rounds conducted via the official CSAB portal (https://csab.nic.in). This year CSAB-Special and DASA (Direct Admission of Students Abroad) have been brought under one umbrella. DASA & CSAB-Special 2026 shall have two rounds of seat allocation. Interested candidates in CSAB-Special 2026 must complete fresh registration after completion of JoSAA rounds, noting that all processes – including registration, choice filling/locking, seat allotment, and fee payment – differ from JoSAA procedures. The document verification for DASA candidates shall be done prior to seat allotment. The same for CSAB candidates shall be done during the rounds. For more information, please visit: https://csab.nic.in
Reservation of seats is as per the rules of Govt. of India, as allocated through the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) 2026 and CSAB Special 2026.

The admission to M. Tech. degree programmes at NIT Uttarakhand is done through Centralized counselling for M. Tech. / M. Arch. / M.Plan. / M. Des. Admissions (CCMT) and admissions are conducted on the basis of valid GATE score. For the admission in the Self Sponsored Category, candidates are shortlisted for written test on the basis of required qualification and the qualified candidates are called for personal interview for final selection.
The Institute provides facilities for research leading to the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Engineering, Science, Humanities and Social Sciences in various disciplines. For the admission in the Ph.D. program, candidates are shortlisted for written test on the basis of required qualification (GATE/NET/SET qualified). The qualified candidates are called for personal interview. After qualifying, the candidates are provisionally admitted for Ph.D. in the Institute. Their registration for Ph.D. program at the Institute is confirmed only after successful completion of Pre-Registration viva-voce.
NIT Uttarakhand has opened its doors for working professionals. These programmes are designed to enable them to pursue higher study while continuing in their Job/Profession in order to help them to accelerate their career.
